ABOUT AI TOOLBOX

Practical technology knowledge, without the noise.

AI ToolBox is an independent technology publication covering useful AI tools, coding workflows, career preparation and digital productivity.

Who publishes AI ToolBox?

AI ToolBox is maintained by Sagar Jadhav, an independent technology publisher and developer focused on practical AI, software development, career preparation and productivity.

The publication is built around a simple standard: a guide should help a reader make a decision or complete a real task, not just repeat a list of popular tools.

What we publish

We publish practical guides for students, developers, job seekers and professionals. Articles aim to explain what a tool is useful for, where it falls short, how to use it responsibly, and when a free option is enough.

  • AI tools, workflows and comparisons
  • Coding and developer productivity
  • Career, resume and placement preparation
  • Study, research and productivity systems

How we research and update articles

For product and pricing claims, we prefer the provider's official documentation, pricing pages and product pages. We compare those claims with the practical use case discussed in the article and avoid presenting marketing language as independent fact.

Articles are reviewed when material product details change. If a claim becomes outdated, we update the relevant section rather than silently changing the recommendation. Published dates and update dates are shown where appropriate.

Originality and usefulness

AI ToolBox does not aim to publish automatically generated pages for search traffic. Each guide is structured around a reader problem, includes limitations and decision criteria, and links readers to primary sources when a claim depends on a third-party product.

AI can be used during drafting or research, but published material is reviewed and edited for clarity, accuracy and usefulness. Readers should independently verify important technical, financial, legal or career decisions.

Corrections and reader feedback

If you spot an incorrect price, outdated feature, broken link or factual mistake, please contact the publisher. Corrections are reviewed and material errors are updated on the relevant page.

Independence & transparency

AI ToolBox may use advertising, affiliate links or sponsored content to support the publication. Commercial relationships will be disclosed clearly and do not automatically determine editorial recommendations.

Who this is for

The publication is designed for students, developers, job seekers, professionals and curious readers who want practical technology guidance without unnecessary jargon.

Get in touch

For editorial feedback, corrections, partnerships or privacy questions, visit the publisher's GitHub profile or use the contact page.
