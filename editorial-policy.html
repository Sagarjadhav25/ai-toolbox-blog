Editorial Policy

How AI ToolBox creates, reviews and updates practical technology content.

Our purpose

AI ToolBox publishes practical guides about AI, software development, careers and productivity. The aim is to help readers understand tools and workflows they can evaluate and use themselves.

How articles are researched

Articles are planned around a specific reader problem. We use product documentation, official resources, direct testing where practical, and established technical references. Time-sensitive details such as pricing, availability and product features can change, so readers should verify current terms with the provider before making decisions.

How we use AI

AI tools may be used during brainstorming, outlining, drafting, editing, example generation and technical exploration. AI output is not treated as automatically correct. Technical claims, code examples and important recommendations are reviewed before publication when practical.

Originality and useful detail

We aim to add practical context rather than republish generic summaries. Articles may include workflows, examples, checklists, comparisons, limitations and verification steps. We do not intentionally pad articles with repetitive text simply to increase length.

Corrections and updates

If an article contains an error or becomes outdated, we may update it and revise its modified date. Readers can report factual or technical issues through the contact page. Corrections are evaluated and incorporated when appropriate.

Reviews and recommendations

When an article discusses a tool or service, descriptions and documented features are kept distinct from editorial commentary. Availability, pricing, policies and product capabilities can change. We do not claim personal testing or firsthand experience where none occurred.

Advertising and affiliate transparency

AI ToolBox may display advertising, including Google AdSense, if the site is approved and eligible. Advertising is separate from editorial decisions. Advertisers do not determine the factual content or conclusions of articles. If affiliate relationships are introduced in the future, relevant commercial relationships will be disclosed on affected pages.

Reader feedback

For corrections, suggestions or questions about an article, use the Contact page. We welcome specific feedback that helps make the site more accurate and useful.
